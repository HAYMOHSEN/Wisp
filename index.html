Wisp – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Applies to Wisp 1.0 and later

Wisp does not collect, store or send any personal data. Your files move directly between your own devices over your local network and never touch the internet or any Jotech server.

1. Who we are

Wisp – Offline AirDrop for Windows is published by Jotech (Amman, Jordan). Contact: haymohsen@gmail.com.

2. What Wisp does with your data

  • File contents are streamed directly from the sending device to the receiving device on your local network and written only to the folder you chose on the receiving PC. Jotech never sees, stores or relays them.
  • File names and sizes are shown in the app's transfer list on your devices only. They are not transmitted anywhere else.
  • Device names (for example "Hani's iPhone", "Office Laptop") are exchanged between your devices so you can pick a target, and are kept on your own PC only.
  • The pairing code and your settings (save folder, language, theme, trusted PCs) are stored in a local settings file on your PC.

3. What Wisp does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No internet connection is made by the app. Wisp communicates only with devices on your local network (Wi‑Fi / LAN).
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

4. Network access

Wisp runs a small server on your PC so that your phone's browser and other PCs can reach it on the local network. On first launch Windows asks you to allow Wisp through the firewall; choose Private networks. Access from other devices requires the pairing code shown in Wisp or, for other PCs, your explicit acceptance in a dialog. Traffic stays on your local network and is not encrypted in transit; use Wisp on networks you trust (your home or office Wi‑Fi), not on public hotspots.

5. The phone web page

The page your phone opens is served by your own PC, not by a website. It stores the pairing code and the device name in your phone browser's local storage so you do not have to scan again. Clearing the browser's site data removes them. The page sets no cookies and loads nothing from the internet.

6. Children

Wisp does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Microsoft Store

If you install Wisp from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process purchase and installation data under its own privacy statement. Jotech receives only the aggregate, anonymous statistics Microsoft provides to app publishers.

8. Your rights

Because Wisp holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for Jotech to access, correct or delete. All data the app keeps lives on your own devices and can be removed by uninstalling the app and deleting the Wisp folder in your Downloads folder.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Wisp ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Material changes will also be noted in the Store "What's new" text.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: haymohsen@gmail.com

يشغّل Wisp خادمًا صغيرًا على حاسوبك ليتمكن متصفح هاتفك والحواسيب الأخرى من الوصول إليه على الشبكة المحلية. عند أول تشغيل يطلب ويندوز السماح لـ Wisp عبر جدار الحماية؛ اختر «الشبكات الخاصة». يتطلب الوصول من الأجهزة الأخرى رمز الاقتران الظاهر في Wisp، أو موافقتك الصريحة في مربع حوار عندما يكون المرسِل حاسوبًا آخر. تبقى الحركة على شبكتك المحلية وهي غير مشفّرة أثناء النقل؛ استخدم Wisp على الشبكات التي تثق بها (شبكة منزلك أو مكتبك) وليس على نقاط الاتصال العامة.

٥. صفحة الهاتف

الصفحة التي يفتحها هاتفك يقدّمها حاسوبك نفسه وليس موقعًا على الإنترنت. تحفظ رمز الاقتران واسم الجهاز في التخزين المحلي لمتصفح الهاتف حتى لا تحتاج إلى المسح مجددًا؛ ويزيلهما مسح بيانات الموقع من المتصفح. لا تضع الصفحة أي كوكيز ولا تحمّل أي شيء من الإنترنت.
